Two PhD students from SPARC, Jung-Won and Fengxu, took part in the [NEAR Frontiers 2026 Workshop](https://mimarlik.itu.edu.tr/en/news-detail/2026/07/23/near-frontiers-2026-symposium-workshop) in Istanbul, held 30 July to 21 August 2026. The symposium and workshop were built around the central topic of neuroarchitecture, exploring how insights from neuroscience and cognitive science can inform the design and understanding of built environments.

The three-week intensive program translated interdisciplinary research questions into hands-on experimental studies, working through research design, EEG and VR-based data collection, preprocessing, and statistical analysis. Great experience connecting with researchers working at the intersection of architecture, neuroscience, and cognitive science!
