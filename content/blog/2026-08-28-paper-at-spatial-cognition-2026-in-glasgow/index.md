Jakub presented the paper **"How Floor Rotation Fragments 3D Cognitive Maps"** at [Spatial Cognition 2026](https://www.gla.ac.uk/research/az/spatialreasoning/sc26/), hosted by the Turner Kirk Centre for Spatial Reasoning at the University of Glasgow, 25–28 August 2026.

The paper is based on the MSc thesis by Amirtha Varshini Raja — great work, Amirtha! The study is part of the [3D Sketch Maps](/projects/3d-sketch-maps/) project, and was conducted together with Tianyi Xiao and Martin Raubal (ETH Zürich) and Angela Schwering.

Using VR navigation and 3D sketch mapping, we found that rotating the floors of a multilevel building leaves within-floor memory almost untouched. However, it reduces the odds of correctly recalling relations across floors. People rely on separate floor-wise representations instead of a unified 3D cognitive map.
