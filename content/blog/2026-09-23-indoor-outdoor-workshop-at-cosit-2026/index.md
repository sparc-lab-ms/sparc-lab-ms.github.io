At [COSIT 2026](https://www.cosit2026.uk/) in York, the team of the In-n-Out Project organised the thematic workshop session titled **"Indoor-vs-Outdoor Spatial Information: Similarities and Differences in Cognition and Computation"**. About 40 conference participants first reflected on route instructions that cross indoor-outdoor thresholds, and then generalised this beyond navigation contexts.

The session was organised by Jakub and Jungwon from SPARC, together with Marina Smirnova and Panos Mavros from Télécom Paris.
